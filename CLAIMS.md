# Where each number in the paper comes from

Generated by `replication/claims_map.py` from the pins in `scripts/check_manuscript.py`,
run against the manuscript as submitted. Table, figure and section numbers are those of
the submitted manuscript and its Supporting Information (numbers starting with S).
Every row of [`replication/claims.csv`](replication/claims.csv) is one printed figure,
with the value its artifact gives.

- **477 pins**, matched at **495 places** in the text; **477** of those are traced to the artifact they depend on.
- An artifact counts as a source of a claim when changing that artifact (its numbers, its
  records or its labels) changes the value the pin demands; see the script's docstring.
- The other 18 are marked `—`: no perturbation of a single artifact moved them, typically because they rest on a field that cannot change without breaking the computation (an identifier, a version string) or on a protocol document rather than an artifact. `scripts/check_manuscript.py` still pins each of them.
